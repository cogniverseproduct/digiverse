## What DigiVerse changed

- `index.html` is the upstream page with its stylesheet and script inlined and asset paths rewritten to `/event-templates/conference-shift26/img/...`.
- `mapping.json` describes how an organiser's data replaces the sample content (names, dates, venue, speakers, schedule, tickets, sponsors, FAQ).
  Layout, typography, colours and motion are unchanged.
- On a client's site the sample avatars, the venue map illustration and the newsletter sign-up form are not shown, and ticket buttons link only to the booking link the organiser provided.
