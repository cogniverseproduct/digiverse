## What DigiVerse changed

- `index.html` is the upstream page with its stylesheet and script inlined and asset paths rewritten to `/event-templates/wedding-evermore/img/...`.
- `mapping.json` describes how the couple's data replaces the sample content. Layout, typography, colours and motion are unchanged.
- On a client's site the template's RSVP form (which only showed a demo message upstream) is replaced by RSVP buttons that open an email to the organiser, the placeholder photographs and the illustrated map are not shown, and only the couple's own photographs are used.
