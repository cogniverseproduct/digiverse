Bring theWend back.

Forty-one miles of river, from the moor to the sea. Three years ago you couldn't swim in any of it. This is how a town is changing that.

Chapter one · The problem

Every bend of the river had a bottle in it.

Storm drains, a fly-tip under the bypass, and a century of not looking. By 2023 the Wend failed every test the agency ran, and the herons had gone upstream.

4,180 kg

of rubbish lifted out of the Wend since the first clean-up.

Chapter two · The clean-ups

Then people started turning up with gloves.

One Saturday a month, then two. Schools, rowing clubs, the brewery by the weir. Each clean-up is logged, weighed and mapped, so the drains that send the most can be fixed first.

312volunteers this year

Chapter three · What comes back

Last summer, you could see the stones.

Two stretches passed the bathing test in 2026. Trout are back below the mill. The next goal is the whole river, moor to sea, by 2030.
